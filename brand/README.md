## Logo assets

- `logo/suzi-horizontal-primary.svg`: primary on light backgrounds

Use the forest/cream pairing for primary text. Reserve gold for accents and the i-dot. Maintain clear space; do not distort, add portraits, or alter the brand mark without approval.

The magnolia illustration and finished PNG/PDF variants are preserved in a controlled archival brand kit, not included in this minimal public export. This is an intentionally bounded asset release; the full asset kit has a separately verified SHA-256 checksum.
