WHY SUZI EXISTS

Built around people, not prompts.

SUZI is named in honor of a real person whose experiences inspired a simple principle: advanced technology should not demand advanced technical knowledge from the people who need it.

We are exploring small, portable AI approaches, grounded information retrieval, and user-controlled explanations that make complex resources easier to navigate.
